Common questions

Should the same Denver company inspect and remediate?

IICRC S520 treats assessment and remediation as separate roles. Colorado does not forbid one firm from doing both. Ask which visit you are buying.

Does Colorado license mold inspectors?

Not at the state level. CDPHE publishes consumer mold guidance. Ask for a credential you can look up and the name of the lab.

Is a free inspection a good deal?

A free visit that becomes the same company’s cleanup quote is the dual-role pitch. An inspect-only firm charges for the report because the report is the product. This site does not set prices.

Who should do clearance after cleanup?

Ask whether post-remediation verification is a third party. Some insurers and lenders want that.

Does every visible patch need containment and tear-out?

Ask for measured area, the moisture source, and whether the bid is clean-in-place or demolition. EPA and CDPHE consumer pages treat small patches differently from large or hidden growth.
