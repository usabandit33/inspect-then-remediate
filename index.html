A Denver mold visit is two jobs. Name inspect-only testing vs a company that also sells the cleanup before anyone opens a wall.

Households mix a paid independent report with a free inspection that ends as a remediation quote from the same firm.

Ask who writes the scope, who profits if the scope grows, which lab reads the samples, and who returns for clearance.

  • Ask whether the company only inspects and tests, or also sells remediation.
  • Ask who writes the work plan and whether that writer is paid more if more material comes out.
  • Ask which independent lab analyzes samples and whether an outdoor control cassette is included.
  • Ask who does post-remediation clearance — a third party, or the same crew.
  • Colorado does not license mold inspectors. Ask for a look-up credential.
  • Call a shop that publishes a phone on its own website.
